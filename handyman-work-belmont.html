Working in Belmont

It is a mixed town to work in. Belmont Hill runs to large older houses with the upkeep that implies, while Waverley and the streets around Cushing Square are denser and carry more two-families. The work follows the housing: bigger exterior and carpentry jobs on one side of town, steady small repairs on the other.

Being central to the service area matters more than it sounds. Belmont usually sits between two other jobs rather than at the end of a long drive, which is part of why small jobs here are worth taking at all.
