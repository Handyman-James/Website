Snow in Belmont

Belmont Hill is the reason to think about this before December. Steep driveways ice rather than simply filling with snow, which makes deicing as important as clearing, and makes a plow's habit of leaving a compacted layer behind a genuine hazard rather than an annoyance.

A plow on a sloped drive tends to leave a compacted layer behind it that then freezes. Clearing to the surface and treating it afterwards is the difference between a driveway you can walk down in January and one you cannot.

Plows are quick, and they are also what scrapes up driveway surfaces, tears lawn edges and buries mailboxes. Every property on this register is cleared with snowblowers. It takes longer. Nothing underneath gets wrecked.
