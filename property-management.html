What it costs

Property Care

$59 per month, per property. This covers 24-hour access, seasonal walkthroughs, and removes emergency callout fees. Landlords with several properties pay per property.

Home Watch

Added to Property Care for the months you're away, and quoted per home — the price depends on how often the house needs visiting.

The work itself

Billed at James's standard hourly rate, plus materials and any other costs incurred. The membership isn't a labour plan — it's what makes sure someone knows the property, someone answers, and that calling at midnight doesn't cost more than calling at noon.

The maths

A single after-hours emergency callout from a contractor typically carries a premium of several hundred dollars before anyone picks up a tool. A year of Property Care costs about what one or two of those would — and with it, the 3am call costs the same as the Tuesday one.
