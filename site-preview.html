Frequently asked questions

Is Handyman James licensed and insured?

Yes. James holds an unrestricted Massachusetts Construction Supervisor License (CSL #120385) and is a registered Home Improvement Contractor (HIC #197410) — the state registration that gives clients access to the Guaranty Fund and arbitration program if something goes wrong. He also carries insurance. If a job falls outside his license, he'll say so before starting the work, not after.

What areas does Handyman James serve?

Newton, Lexington, Arlington, Waltham, Lincoln, Concord, Wellesley, Belmont, Cambridge, and Boston, Massachusetts. He's based in Newton. If you're just outside these areas, it's still worth asking.

What's the difference between a handyman and a general contractor?

A handyman typically handles smaller repairs and maintenance jobs, while a general contractor manages larger construction projects that require permits and specialty licensing. James holds an unrestricted Massachusetts Construction Supervisor License, which reflects that broader qualification, even though his day-to-day work is handyman and property management.

Is there emergency or 24/7 handyman service available?

Yes, for property management clients — both homeowners and rental property owners. James offers 24/7 on-call support alongside routine seasonal maintenance.

How does snow removal work — do you use a plow?

No. James clears snow and ice using snow blowers only, for both residential and commercial properties. It's slower than plowing, but it avoids the driveway and lawn damage a plow can cause.

Is any job too small?

No. James takes on jobs many contractors won't bother with, from hanging a single picture to repairing old machinery. If it's a job that's outside his license or skills, he'll tell you upfront.

How much does a handyman cost in the Newton area?

It depends on the scope of the job. The most accurate way to get a number is to describe the work directly — contact James with details and he'll give you a straight answer.
