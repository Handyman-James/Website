What's the difference between a handyman and a general contractor?

A handyman typically handles smaller repairs and maintenance jobs, while a general contractor manages larger construction projects that require permits and specialty licensing. James holds an unrestricted Massachusetts Construction Supervisor License, which covers both — general repairs and larger renovation projects.

Is there emergency or 24/7 handyman service available?

How does snow removal work — do you use a plow?

No. James clears snow and ice using snow blowers only, for both residential and commercial properties. It's slower than plowing, but it avoids the driveway and lawn damage a plow can cause.

Is any job too small?
