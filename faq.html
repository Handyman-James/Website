What's the difference between a handyman and a general contractor?

A handyman typically handles smaller repairs and maintenance jobs, while a general contractor manages larger construction projects that require permits and specialty licensing. James holds an unrestricted Massachusetts Construction Supervisor License, which reflects that broader qualification, even though his day-to-day work is handyman and property management.
